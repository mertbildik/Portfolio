## Problem

Bunect was growing mainly through word of mouth.
There was no website to explain services clearly.
There was no deck to present the offer in meetings.

**Context**

Core problem statement
The service was real, but there was no clear digital path to educate and convert new clients.

**Objectives**

- Make services easy to understand
- Make contact one tap away

**User scenario:** People had to ask everything in chat.
That slowed down decisions and cost leads.

## Approach

Services were grouped into clear categories.
Content was written from real client questions.
A landing flow was built to push high intent users to action fast.

## Solution

The goal was simple.
Let people understand the offer in seconds and reach the team instantly.

### Key decisions

**Landing page website** — Clear service categories. Short explanations for each service. Contact form for warm leads.

**One click conversion path** — A direct button that opens WhatsApp in one tap. Built for fast mobile decisions.

**Sales presentation deck** — A deck for meetings and outreach. Clear structure. Easy to skim.

**Brand refinement** — A base identity existed. The system was improved and made more consistent across touchpoints.

## Output

### Website

A single landing page that explains services and converts traffic into inquiries. Built to work with social and Google Ads.

- Images: `home`, `services`, `contact`

### Presentation deck

A reusable deck for client meetings and outreach.

- Images: `presentation-1`, `presentation-2`

### Brand system

Cleaner visuals and more consistent use of type and color.

## Impact

**User**

- **+20 WhatsApp clicks per month** — People don’t hunt for info. They tap once and ask the right question.
- **3 Clear service paths** — Accounting, TRC, and company setup each has a direct route. People land, pick, and move.

**Business**

- **+2000 Website visits per month** — A steady stream of intent in one place. Easier to answer questions and convert interest.
- **+8 Meetings booked per month** — The deck and site make the first conversation shorter, clearer, and easier to lead.

## Retrospective

Small businesses do not need complex funnels.
They need clear offers and fast contact paths.
