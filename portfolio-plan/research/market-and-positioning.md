# Market and positioning — who this portfolio should attract

Hiring-market research (Poland + international remote + client work), Aug 2026, plus what it
implies for positioning. Sources are current job listings and primary company pages.

## Poland / CEE, right now

- Warsaw in-house range: **12–22k PLN/mo** for product designers (justjoin.it, Aug 2026 —
  AirHelp 18–27k, Future Mind 16–20k, SINGU 12.4–20k, etc.); senior aggregate ~155–240k+
  PLN/yr (Uxcel 2026 guide, levels.fyi). Contract/B2B: 100–150 PLN/h.
- The title "**AI Design Engineer**" now exists on the Polish board (Upside, Warsaw).
- Design-respected companies here: ElevenLabs (remote-first, Warsaw office, hires both
  Product Designer and Design Engineer), Booksy, DocPlanner, Ramp Network; agency lane is
  led by Tonik (Poznań), which sells to YC/VC-backed US SaaS (LangChain, Clay, Okta).
- 2026 salary premiums go to: AI product design, design-systems depth, data visualization.

## The roles this profile actually maps to

The interesting finding: the profile maps *above* the standard Warsaw board, onto two role
shapes that pay differently and screen differently.

- **Design engineer** (Ashby €58–133k remote EU; ElevenLabs remote incl. Warsaw; Vercel,
  Cursor). What the JDs say: "design, build, and ship autonomously"; portfolio of "projects
  (personal or professional) with at least a couple of users that showcase great visual
  taste"; ElevenLabs wants "compelling web experiences for marketing websites… strong enough
  to go viral." **And the warning shot, verbatim from Ashby**: this role is "**not a Designer
  vibe coding prototypes**" — real engineering judgment required.
- **Founding designer** (startup roles, 0.5–1% equity): brand + product + website in one
  person, "structuring chaos," strategy included. This is the closest one-to-one description
  of what the OFK project actually was.
- **Senior product designer** at product companies: end-to-end ownership, prototypes in code
  now normal (Intercom lists Framer/Lovable/code as designer tools; Photoroom mandates
  "leverage AI tools to work smarter").

## AI in hiring — settled, with one edge

- Explicitly named in JDs: Anthropic's own designer JD expects candidates "already using
  Claude Code or similar"; Photoroom, Intercom, Perplexity similar. Fountain Institute: 73% of
  design managers call AI proficiency essential; **absence of AI signals now reads as
  avoidance**.
- The only red flag is AI-generated portfolio *content* — generic case studies, buzzwords, no
  specifics. "Ships with AI" is safe exactly when paired with what was directed, rejected,
  and verified. This confirms the master plan's framing and raises its priority.

## What screens candidates out (2025–26 hiring commentary)

Reams of process text · template stronger than the work · weak first visual impression ("the
number one reason people don't get interviews") · no evidence of shipping · double-diamond
diagrams as thinking · unclear personal ownership · unverifiable metrics · Notion-template
portfolios · stale work (>2 years old). The current live portfolio fails several of these
(template case studies, unverifiable numbers) — the master plan's diagnosis is confirmed
externally.

## Client work — the lane and the tiers

- Comparables: Tonik at $15–40k/project ($50–99/h, min $10k); basement.studio as the
  aspirational tier (Vercel, Cursor, ElevenLabs as clients) — that lane is won on visible
  taste + technical credibility, not service lists. Commodity Framer/Webflow builds sit at
  $500–5k. Fractional design retainers run $3–10k/mo and demand grew ~68% YoY.
- The buyer who pays independent-premium is a funded B2B founder buying senior judgment +
  speed + one accountable person. They shop by visible craft — the portfolio site itself is
  the first exhibit, and speed-via-AI is a stated advantage over agency timelines.

## The positioning question this raises (for Mert to decide, not this file)

The master plan decided: one label, **Product Designer**, with design engineering as stated
direction. The hiring research pushes back: the rare, better-paying demand is at the
founding-designer / design-engineer intersection, and this profile's actual shape (brand +
system + shipped code, end to end) matches those JDs almost word for word.

Both positions have evidence. The case for holding the plan's line: Ashby's "not a Designer
vibe coding prototypes" bar is real, the engineering artifact isn't there yet, and the plan
already documents "revisit after a project with heavier engineering surface." The case for
moving: staying "Product Designer" self-sorts into the 12–22k PLN Warsaw board, while the
work itself already answers the founding-designer JD.

**A middle path the evidence supports without a title change**: keep "Product Designer" as
the label, but make the hero's *claim* the end-to-end fact ("I design and build…" — already
decided) and let the work section's live links carry the design-engineer signal. That is, the
title stays conservative while every artifact reads at the higher tier. This is what Rauno
does in reverse (works at Vercel-level, calls himself "interaction designer") — underclaiming
the title while overdelivering the evidence is itself a recognized senior pattern.
