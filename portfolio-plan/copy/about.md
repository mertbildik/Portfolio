## The story

Born in Izmir. Based in Warsaw.

As a kid, I spent hours on the computer, and not only for fun. I liked figuring out simple
ways to fix everyday problems, and that habit never really left.

The path since then hasn't been a straight line, and I've stopped pretending it should be.
I started a computer science degree at Warsaw University of Technology, switched after a
year, and graduated in International Relations from the University of Warsaw. Somewhere in
between, design quietly became the thing I did with all my spare attention.

In the second half of 2021, two things started weeks apart. I joined Adclusive, a Swedish
ad platform, as their part-time product designer, before the company was even formally
incorporated. A month later I joined McKinsey as a visual communication specialist: three
years of turning dense analysis into pages executives could act on. Day job and side
product ran in parallel until Adclusive wound down in late 2024. I didn't think of it as a
plan at the time. Looking back, it was one: communication rigor from one, product reality
from the other.

Since late 2024 I've been independent, running my own company (Curvix on paper; the work
speaks under its own names). That's where the current chapter started: AI tools got good
enough that I could carry a design all the way to the live site myself. OFK and Sinerjik
are what that looks like in practice.

I'll be straight about the engineering side: I'm still learning it, deliberately and in
order. The long-term direction is design engineering, designing and building as one job.
The sites on this page are the milestones so far, not the destination.

## How I work

Short version, since the case studies show the long one:

- **Facts first.** On OFK, the first file in the repository sorts what we could prove from
  what we couldn't. Every project since gets the same treatment.
- **Rules before pages.** Design tokens and written rules come before layouts, so the system
  keeps me honest when the deadline gets loud.
- **Measure, don't guess.** If a decision can have a number (contrast, width, milliseconds),
  it gets one before it ships.
- **AI in the loop, on the record.** I build with Claude Code and I'll tell you exactly how.
  The direction, rules, and rejections are mine. The typing mostly isn't.

## Off the clock

Basketball and football keep me hooked. I also co-run an English-language Galatasaray fan
page, which is its own kind of product work. Design is where I feel most at home.
